5 reports read as a one-day move; filed under parser by tenant.

parser

The shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so a booking for 2 March comes out as 3 February. This covers app and email reports from every tenant, and form reports from tenants on booking-v2. Ticket #1 covers this row except the reports in the note under the table.

hint

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. A parser fix leaves these broken. The hint has to change. Only tenants on booking-v3 who book through the form hit it. The table above still marks this row as having no ticket of its own. It has one now: #2.

one-day move

The reports in the note under the table have no day and month swapped. The booking lands one day early, or on a different day depending on where the reader is: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That points at time zones, not at the parser. The desk files them under parser by tenant and counts them there, so the parser row includes them. Ticket #3 covers the reports in the note, and only those.

The tenant and the channel decide a report’s cause (causes.ts). Intake’s tag guesses from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the cause often, so this page does not use it.
