parser

The shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so a booking for 2 March comes out as 3 February. This covers app and email reports from every tenant, and form reports from tenants on booking-v2.

hint

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. A parser fix leaves these broken. The hint has to change. Only tenants on booking-v3 who book through the form hit it.

oneDayMove

None of these reports has a day and month swapped. The booking lands one day early, or on a different day depending on where the reader is: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That points at time zones, not at the parser. The desk listed them in desk-notes.json as a one-day move. The #4471 page counts them inside parser and footnotes them. This page gives them their own row, so its parser row is smaller than #4471’s by exactly that row.

The tenant and the channel decide a report’s cause (causes.ts). Intake’s tag guesses from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the cause often, so this page does not use it.
