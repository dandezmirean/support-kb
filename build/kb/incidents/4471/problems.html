parser: #4471

The shared parser reads every date month first. All our tenants write the day first, so a booking for 2 March comes out as 3 February. This covers app and email reports from every tenant, and form reports from tenants on booking-v2.

hint: #2

The hint row above. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type the month first, as the hint tells them, and the form reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. A parser fix leaves these broken. The hint has to change. Only tenants on booking-v3 who book through the form hit it. The table still says this row has no ticket of its own, because the export predates #2.

one-day move: #5

The note under the table. These reports have no day and month swapped. The booking lands one day early, or on a different day depending on where the reader is: “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” That points at time zones, not at the parser. The desk files them under parser by tenant and channel (causes.ts), so the table counts them in that row.
